regenerated by `python3 -m scorecard.run`.

## Out of scope for v1 (deliberate)
- Spec → circuit synthesis family (planned v2; needs a hardened open-ended checker).
- Signal/power integrity, EMI, thermal.
- Real manufacturer part numbers (v1 uses generic parts with self-contained datasheets to
  avoid datasheet-accuracy and licensing problems).
- KiCad 8/9 file formats (container has KiCad 7; port is mechanical once 9 is available).
